# Ilustración de bienvenida

Creada con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Archivo del proyecto: `public/assets/companions.png`. La animación suave se aplica con CSS y se desactiva con la preferencia de reducir movimiento.

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: login welcome illustration for a cozy private reading library. Primary request: an extremely cute animated-cartoon style anthropomorphic little ivory bone and a little green broccoli, sitting close together reading one open cream book. The bone has the unmistakable rounded dog-bone silhouette but is a friendly character, the broccoli has a fluffy forest-green crown and short light-green stalk body. Both have tiny warm smiling faces, black dot eyes, rosy cheeks, little arms and feet. Soft hand-painted clay-like 3D storybook illustration, tasteful sage, ivory and muted blush palette, warm diffuse light, cozy tender mood, clean polished detail. Centered full-body duo with ample breathing room, square composition. No lettering, no logos, no watermarks. Transparent background, isolated characters and their shared book, soft grounding shadow only.
